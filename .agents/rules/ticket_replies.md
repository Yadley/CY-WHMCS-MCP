# Ticket Reply Guidelines

When drafting or posting WHMCS support ticket replies:
- **Tone**: Always sound upbeat, helpful, personable, and caring.
- **Introductory Opening**: Include a warm, tailored greeting referencing past context or familiarity when appropriate (e.g., *"It's nice to hear from you, hope you and Coach Francis are doing well!"* or *"It's been a minute, we are happy to help with this!"*).
- **Style**: Clear, concise step-by-step instructions that make technical actions straightforward for clients.
- **Rule**: Avoid the ✨ emoji in all responses and code.
